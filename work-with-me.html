Work with me

Roles I'm open to

Permanent product manager

A full-time role in a product team, owning a product from discovery through to release.

Contract product manager or product owner

I own a defined area for a few months, run the backlog and keep the team and stakeholders aligned.

Part-time product lead

One to three days a week for a team without a senior product person, including as its sole product person.

How I work

Discovery to delivery

I work across the whole product lifecycle, from customer research and prototyping to release and go-to-market.

Method agnostic

I adapt the framework to the team and the project, not the other way round.

Customers and data first

I talk to users and look at the numbers before deciding what to build.

Clear communication

Stakeholders and the team hear the same thing, up and down, with risks raised early.

Where I fit

A good fit

B2B software, hardware plus software, mobile apps, web portals, access control, security and IoT.

Probably not a fit

Finance and insurance, telecommunications and other domains that take a long time to learn, when there is no room to get up to speed first. I would rather say so and point you to someone better.
